# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Keys Tools welcoming, safe, and equitable.
Everyone who participates in good faith receives the same privileges,
regardless of identity, background, experience, ability, or status.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, honest, and respectful.
- Respect different viewpoints and clearly expressed boundaries.
- Give and receive constructive feedback without personal attacks.
- Credit sources and protect private information.
- Take responsibility, repair harm, and keep technical disagreement focused on
  the work.

Harassment, discrimination, sexualization, threats, impersonation, deliberate
misinformation, disclosure of private information, and sustained disruptive or
commercial promotion are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report project-specific incidents privately through the repository's
[private report form](https://github.com/keys-i/tools/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `Conduct:`. Do not post identifying details in a
public issue. GitHub-wide abuse can also be reported through
[GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Maintainers will protect reporter confidentiality as far as practical,
investigate fairly, and choose a proportionate response. Responses may include
a private warning, temporary participation limits, suspension, or a permanent
ban. Anyone handling a report in which they are involved must recuse
themselves.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents
the project.
